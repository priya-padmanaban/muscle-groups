# Third-party anatomical assets

## BodyExplorer anatomy atlas

- Asset: `public/models/bodyparts3d-anatomy.glb`
- Mesh metadata: `public/models/bodyparts3d-mesh-mapping.json`
- Prepared by: Johan Bellander
- Source: https://github.com/JohanBellander/BodyExplorer
- Included structures: 467 muscle, tendon, and connective-tissue meshes
- Modifications in Kinetic Atlas: runtime axis/scale normalization, normal and bounding-volume regeneration, logical muscle classification, rigid segment attachment, activation materials
